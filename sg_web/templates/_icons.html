{# The application's marks, drawn once. They were HTML entities: ♥ ★ × □ ⓘ ▶ ▾. Those are not icons, they are whatever glyph the reader's system font happens to ship at that codepoint -- different stroke weights, different optical sizes and different baselines from each other on one machine, and different again on the next. `□` is WHITE SQUARE standing in for "lights out", `ⓘ` is a circled letter, and `×` was doing three unrelated jobs at once: clear a rating, remove a keyword, close the window. One geometry: a 16 box, 1.5 stroke, round caps and joins, currentColor throughout so a mark takes the ink of whatever it sits in. `filled` for the two that carry a state in their body rather than beside it -- a favourite and a star are on or off, and an outline that only changes colour reads as disabled rather than as unset. aria-hidden on every one: each sits beside its own word, or inside a control that already carries an aria-label. A mark that announced itself would say the name twice. #} {% macro icon(name, filled=false) %}{% endmacro %}
